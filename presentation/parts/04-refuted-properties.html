Minnow: the rule

  • n ≥ 3f + 1; no self-parent rule.
  • Commit a leader block l when n − f processes reference it from the round above, and every earlier leader slot is resolved: one of its blocks is in l's history, or is concurrent with l and committed or skipped.
  • Skip when n − f processes of the round above have no block that references it.
  • The rule consults no later anchor.

A commit needs a quorum in the round above and every earlier slot resolved; the rule reads no later anchor.

Minnow.ValidHere (LeanDag/Minnow/Model/Rule.lean:43); Quorum (:119); Skipped (:134; the vertex-counting reading SkippedByVertex, :145, lets one vertex be committed and skipped, LeanDagTest/Minnow/Skip.lean:60, 69); CommittedAt (:161).

Minnow: an earlier leader committed after a later one

n = 4. Process 0 equivocates at round 0: blocks 0 and 4. Block 4 is referenced by process 1 only.

  • A validator missing block 5 sees the round-1 leader block 6 reach block 4, which resolves slot 0. Block 6 is committed, while slot 0 is still undecided.
  • Block 5 arrives. Block 0 now has a quorum and is committed for slot 0, after the later leader.

Initial state: the twins 0 and 4 and their references, in the partial view. Step 1: the partial view commits block 6. Step 2: blocks 5 and 12 arrive; the full view commits block 0 for the earlier slot.

Minnow: slots that are never decided

The Byzantine process's blocks reach two processes of four: too few to commit, too many to skip. A slot that is resolved only by a block in a leader's history holds later leaders for two rounds.

Black Marlin: the rule

  • n ≥ 3f + 1; one anchor per round; no certificates.
  • Commit an anchor B at round r when n − f validators reference it at round r + 1, and some anchor at r + 1 references B and is itself referenced by n − f validators at r + 2.
  • There is no skip. Anchors that are not committed are output through a descent from a later committed anchor.

Three recurring lessons

  1. Fix an order. Wherever two twins can pass a test, the choice between them must be made by an order every validator computes.
  2. Count, do not infer from absence. A skip must count blocks that exist.
  3. Use later evidence. Every rule proved safe here has an indirect rule that reads a later committed anchor; the two refuted rules have none.

  • 3: every carrier has rungs ≥ 1; Black Marlin and Minnow have no anchor rule. (informal) That the missing rule causes the failures.
Part III

Properties

From rules to properties

Mechanisms such as garbage collection or crash recovery should not be proved once per rule. Instead:

  • each rule is proved to have a few properties;
  • each mechanism is proved once, using only those properties.

Eleven rules (all but Minnow and Black Marlin) have the properties.

The diagram of slide 2, with the properties highlighted. The eleven carriers are listed in the notes of slide 2.

The carrier

A mechanism accesses a rule only through its carrier:

  • a type of universes (all blocks) and of views (what one validator holds);
  • the round, creator and references of each block;
  • the decision relation: under schedule S, view V decides slot k as v.

Laws: a view holds only universe blocks; a view holds every block that its blocks reference; references go one round down.

DagRule (LeanDag/Properties/Carrier.lean:31); Decided is a relation Slots → View U → ℕ → Option BlockId → Prop (:56); viewSound (:45), viewComplete (:49), causal (:54). Eleven carriers (slide 2).

Banded: a verdict reads a finite band of rounds

Each verdict depends only on the blocks in a band of rounds, from the slot's round to some round top. Any other universe, view and schedule that match in the band — up to a shift of round and slot numbers — reach the same verdict.

leader blockvotecertificate

The band of the slot of block 11 is rounds 3–5 of Uexcl and rounds 1–3 of the cut; the verdict is the same in both.

Agree, CommitsCandidate, Indirect

  • Agree: two views of one universe never decide a slot differently, skips included.
  • CommitsCandidate: a committed block is a candidate of its slot.
  • Indirect: a committed eligible anchor, with every eligible slot in between skipped, decides the slot; changing the other slots' leaders does not change that verdict.

Agree (LeanDag/Properties/Agree.lean:22); CommitsCandidate (LeanDag/Properties/Candidate.lean:50); Indirect (LeanDag/Properties/Commit.lean:40). Indirect gives Descends (Descends.of_indirect, LeanDag/Properties/Derived/Descent.lean:83).

The support: the conditions for a commit

  • A support names the wave and what it means for a block to certify a candidate.
  • Law 1 (local): certification depends only on the wave's rounds, so it is unchanged by cutting or shifting rounds outside them.
  • Law 2 (commits): if a reliable quorum T leads a slot, produces through its wave and certifies every candidate, then a view that holds those rounds commits the slot.
  • Liveness needs exactly this, at every slot T leads. The network assumption is used only to establish it.

Support (LeanDag/Properties/Support.lean:39), Local (:92), Commits (:103). Optional and derived properties

Optional, needed by one mechanism each:

  • CommitsDirect: a direct commit is a verdict (adaptive leader count).
  • SkipsUnsupported: a slot is skipped when a large enough set, present one round above, references none of its candidates (prompt skip).
  • Quorate: every block references n − f distinct creators (chain quality).
  • SelfParent, NoEquiv: every block references its creator's previous block; a reliable creator makes one block per round (inclusion).
  • CommitsDirect (LeanDag/Properties/Optional/Direct.lean:25), used by LeanDag/Barnacle/Healthy/Statement.lean:101. SkipsUnsupported (Optional/Skip.lean:51). Quorate (Optional/Quorate.lean:33), for non-genesis blocks, n − slack with slack = f in the core reliability. SelfParent, NoEquiv (Optional/SelfParent.lean:23, 28).
  • Derivations: Persist.of_banded, LocalTruncate.of_banded, Support.leaderCommits (Derived/LeaderCommits.lean:50), Descends.of_indirect.
  • Some rules also prove them directly: Mysticeti proves LeaderCommits (leaderCommits_cert, LeanDag/Mysticeti/Properties.lean:534) and derives Law 2 from it (:634); Steelhead's persist (LeanDag/Steelhead/Properties.lean:158).

Safety, for every rule and every mechanism

From Banded, Agree and CommitsCandidate, across any sequence of cuts, recoveries and re-geneses:

  • every verdict above the settling round is the same before and after;
  • any view after agrees with any view before, above the settling round;
  • every commit is a candidate, and no block is committed at two slots;
  • adding blocks never changes a verdict.

Safe (LeanDag/Properties/Arcs/Headline.lean:46), safety (:63), Safe.prefix_agree (:76). The last bullet is the conjunct on Extends.

Liveness, for every rule

From Law 2, CommitsCandidate, SelfParent and NoEquiv:

  • Progress: every slot below a run of reliably led slots is decided, and reliably led slots continue to be committed.
  • Inclusion: every block of a reliable validator enters the ledger, through a later slot its creator leads.

Seven rules have both. Nemo, FinWhale, Hydrozoan and Optimal-Hydrozoan lack SelfParent and have progress only.
